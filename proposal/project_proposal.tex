\section{Motivation and research question}
Volatility shocks challenge calibrated financial intervals. Neural control, score--threshold adaptation, and retrospective updates already address adaptation \citep{ncc,adapt,retro}. I ask: \textbf{how do current neural scale response and historical score memory jointly affect calibration at shock entry and recovery?} I will investigate dependence on fitted model, training data, and shock duration.

\section{Method and evaluation}
GRU and causal TCN models learn Gaussian scales from raw past returns, with validation early stopping. At zero center, current context $a$ and archive context $b$ give half-width
\[
 h_t^{a,b,W}=s_t^a\,Q_{1-\alpha}\bigl(\{|y_i|/s_i^b\}_{i=t-W}^{t-1}\bigr),\qquad \alpha=0.1.
\]
Cross $a,b\in\{16,64\}$ and score windows $W\in\{32,128\}$, sharing weights within fits; $a=b$ is ordinary calibration. Paired shock/no-shock simulations vary duration, magnitude, tails, and stochastic volatility. Decompose event-minus-control context-by-window contrasts in both current/archive orders. Repeat across five paired seeds and independent training datasets. Controls include EWMA, fitted GARCH, and rolling/adaptive/strongly adaptive calibration \citep{saocp}.

Use generated data and daily log returns from public ECB EUR-cross rates only. The ECB pilot trains through 2018, validates in 2019--2020, and evaluates 2021 onward exploratorily. Report interval score, coverage, width, and simulated oracle deficits; date-block comparisons retain cross-asset dependence. Hyperparameters use validation data; confirmatory simulations use frozen rules and new paths.

\section{Expected contribution and feasibility}
Expected outcomes are a reproducible current/archive decomposition and a phase-specific explanation of \emph{neural fitting variability}. Exploration and a three-dataset replication demonstrate local feasibility and mixed results. Through November, I will consolidate lag/pulse diagnostics, literature positioning, robustness checks, and public financial analysis, then finalize the report and presentation before December 11.
